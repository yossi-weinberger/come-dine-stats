# Time Out season 5–6 dataset lead

## Source

Time Out Tel Aviv published the article **"בדקנו את הסטטיסטיקה: כך תנצחו ב\"בואו לאכול איתי\""** on 2022-09-23, by נעמה רק.

The article says its analysis entered **54 contestants from the two then-recent seasons (5–6)** into an Excel dataset. It describes fields including age, family status, hosting day/order, competition success, meal style/category and several subjective editorial classifications.

## Recovery status

**The spreadsheet itself has not been recovered.**

The currently indexed article exposes the aggregate analysis but no public Google Sheets/XLSX link was found during the 2026-08-11 research pass. Searches for likely spreadsheet/file links and indexed copies did not surface the underlying file.

Do not reconstruct contestant-level rows from article percentages or aggregate claims. Those are useful as a research lead/check, not row-level evidence.

## Useful aggregate clues

The article distinguishes meal categories such as meat, dairy/fish, and vegetarian/vegan and compares average scores across them. Because the published category counts appear internally difficult to reconcile with the stated 54-person sample, preserve the article as-is and do not use those totals as canonical data without the original workbook.

1. Archived versions/assets of the Time Out article.
2. Google Sheets/Drive links embedded in historical HTML or social shares.
3. Static XLS/XLSX/CSV assets on Time Out's media/CDN domains.
